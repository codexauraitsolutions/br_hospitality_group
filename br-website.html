    Our Story
    About BR Hospitality Group

    A legacy of warmth, celebration and unforgettable experiences — built across 15 years and seven extraordinary venues in Hyderabad.

    Who We Are
    A Legacy Built on Passion & Service

    BR Hospitality Group was founded with a single vision — to create spaces where people come together, celebrate life and create memories that last a lifetime. What started as a single restaurant in Champapet has grown into a sprawling hospitality empire spanning fine dining, grand convention centres, luxury resorts, farm stays, club houses and professional catering services.

    Today, our seven distinct verticals across Hyderabad serve over 50,000 guests annually — from intimate family dinners to grand weddings of 3000+ guests. Every venue carries the same DNA: uncompromising quality, heartfelt service and an obsessive attention to detail.
